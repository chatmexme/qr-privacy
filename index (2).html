Privacy Policy

QR Studio – UPI Payments & Any Link to QR
Version 1.0.0 Last Updated: November 13, 2026
Your Privacy Matters. QR Studio is designed with privacy in mind. Your QR code data is processed locally on your device wherever the app’s features permit. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

QR Studio does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to generate QR codes.

Information you enter, including:

  • Payee Name
  • UPI ID / VPA
  • Payment Amount
  • Payment Remarks
  • Website URLs
  • Text and other QR content

is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers by the QR generation feature.

2. Device Permissions Explained

QR Studio may request certain device permissions to provide its features.

Storage / Photos and Media

  • Used when you choose to save a generated QR code or image.
  • Saved images are stored on your device.

Camera

  • Used exclusively for scanning QR codes.
  • Camera access is activated when you use the QR scanning feature.

Clipboard

  • Used only when you explicitly select the Paste option.
  • The app does not continuously monitor your clipboard in the background.

3. No Financial Credentials Handled

QR Studio is a QR code generator, not a banking or payment processing application.

We NEVER request, collect, or store sensitive financial credentials such as:

  • UPI PINs
  • Bank passwords
  • OTPs
  • CVVs
  • Debit or credit card details

Actual payment transactions take place exclusively through your selected official UPI application, such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM.

Important: Generating a payment QR code does not transfer money or initiate a payment.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

QR Studio’s privacy practices depend on the third-party services enabled in the installed version.

If analytics or advertising services are integrated, those providers may process certain device or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not use QR content to build advertising profiles.

5. Data Security & On-Device Processing

We prioritize local processing to minimize unnecessary data transfers.

QR content generated within the app remains on your device unless you explicitly share it through another application or service.

You are responsible for protecting QR images and information you choose to share.

6. Children’s Privacy

QR Studio is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, permissions, or applicable requirements change.

Updates will be reflected in the revised policy version and last updated date within the app.
